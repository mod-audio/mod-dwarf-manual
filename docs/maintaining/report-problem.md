# Reporting a Problem

!!! info "New in 1.14"
    The Report a problem button is new in MOD OS 1.14.

Found a bug, or something that does not behave as it should? The **Report a problem** button gathers the details a good report needs, for you to post on the MOD forum.

1. In the Web UI, click **Report a problem** in the bar at the bottom of the screen. (It is also in Settings.)
2. A window opens with a block of text. Write at the top what you did, what you expected and what happened.
3. Click **Copy to clipboard**.
4. Click **Open the forum topic** and paste the text as a reply.

Nothing is sent by itself: you choose what to post.

## What the text includes

- The MOD OS release, the device and its controller firmware.
- How long the Dwarf has been running and how many audio dropouts (xruns) it counted.
- When you report from the main screen: the pedalboard you have loaded and its plugins.
- The browser you are using.

Your device serial number and the full logs are not included. If the MOD team needs them, they will ask in the topic.
